Each dot is one Instagram post, placed by how many accounts it reached. Hover, tap or tab to a dot for its number. The collab reel (16,711) is left out because five accounts share its reach.

How to read this chart

  • Blue dot. One of the first 10 posts with insights.
  • Pink dot. One of the latest 9 posts.
  • White bar. The median: half the posts reached fewer accounts, half reached more.
  • Faded bar. Where the first median sat, for comparison.
  • Dashed line and shading. The 1,500 benchmark and everything above it.
  • Further right. More accounts reached. Reach counts unique accounts, not views.
